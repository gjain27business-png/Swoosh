What we collect

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile at any time, and delete your account and all data from Safety → Delete my account. You can contact our grievance officer for any request or complaint.

Retention

ID images and selfies: deleted on decision. Account data: until you delete your account. IP logs: up to one year, for safety and legal requests.
