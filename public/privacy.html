Privacy Policy

Draft for beta — to be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

PAUSE is operated by [YOUR COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we"). This notice explains what we collect and why, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

What we collect

  • Mobile number and password (to run your account)
  • Profile details and photos you add (to show you to people in your category)
  • Optional details you choose to add: religion, food preference, height, zodiac, kundli birth time and city, Destiny answers. You can remove any of them at any time.
  • A photo of your ID and a selfie (only to verify you; deleted as soon as we decide)
  • A one-way code made from your ID number (to stop duplicate or banned accounts), plus its last 4 characters (so our reviewer can match it to your ID photo). We cannot read your full ID number from the code, and we never store the full number. For Aadhaar, please upload a masked Aadhaar that shows only the last 4 digits.
  • Chats, likes, reports, help requests and IP addresses (to run the service and keep it safe). Automated checks flag messages that ask for money, OTPs, crypto or contain links, to warn the other person.

Who sees what

With Masked on, your photos stay blurred until you and the other person both unmask. Every photo shown to someone else carries a faint watermark with their account code. Your number is shown only after you both choose to share numbers. Destiny answers are used only for ranking and are never shown on your profile. We never sell your data.

Ask PAUSE (help bot)

The help bot answers from fixed rules and can see only your verification status. It cannot read your chats. If you send a message to our team, a person reads it.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile at any time, take a pause, and delete your account and all data from Safety → Delete my account. You can contact our grievance officer for any request or complaint.

Retention

ID images and selfies: deleted on decision. Account data: until you delete your account. IP logs: up to one year, for safety and legal requests.
