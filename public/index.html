Safety is built in.

Every member verified

The same ID and selfie check for everyone. A banned member’s ID can’t be used to sign up again.

Photo watermark

Every photo you see carries a faint watermark with your account code, so a leaked screenshot can be traced.

Scam warnings

Messages asking for money, OTPs, crypto or containing links are flagged. Numbers stay locked until you both share.

Contact shield

Hide from relatives, former partners and colleagues by number or company. They never see you.

Date plan

Send your plan to someone you trust, share your location in one tap, or call 112.

Take a pause

Hide from picks for a while without losing your chats. Come back whenever you’re ready.

Questions.

Is PAUSE free?

Yes, while we launch in Bangalore and Delhi NCR. If we add paid features later, you’ll see the price before anything is charged.

Who can see my photos?

With Masked on, nobody until you and the other person both tap Unmask. With Masked off, verified members in your category can see them. Every photo shown to someone else carries a watermark with their account code.

Why only five chats?

Five real conversations are worth more than fifty half-hearted ones. The limit is the same for everyone. Close a chat politely and a place opens.

What happens to my ID?

A member of our team checks your ID photo and selfie, then both are deleted. We keep only your verified status and a one-way code of your ID number, so one ID can’t open two accounts.

Do I need a kundli?

No. Kundli is optional and used only in Forever. Destiny is optional too, and you can use PAUSE without either.

Can my family or colleagues see me?

Not if you don’t want them to. Add their numbers or your company to the contact shield and you’ll never see each other.
