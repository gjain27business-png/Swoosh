How it works

Four steps. Zero fakes.

Swoosh is slower than swiping on purpose: a handful of verified, compatible people a day, and a chat where personality comes before photos.

01

Verify in 60 seconds

Government ID and a live selfie, checked by a real person. Then we delete both and keep only a "verified" tick.

02

Only mutual matches see you

You see people who fit your filters, and only if you fit theirs. Same mode, right age, right city.

03

Talk in dark mode

Photos stay blurred while you chat. Numbers and handles can't be shared until you both reach the Contact step.

04

Reveal step by step

One photo, then all photos, then contact. In every match with a woman, she approves each step.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Swoosh really free for women?

Yes. Women never pay. Everyone, women and men, goes through exactly the same ID and selfie verification.

What happens to my ID?

A real person checks your ID photo against your selfie and profile photos. As soon as they decide, both images are deleted. We keep only a "verified" tick, your age, and a one-way code that stops the same ID being used twice. Nobody can read your ID number from it.

Can my family or colleagues see me?

Not if you don't want them to. Add phone numbers to your shield and those people will never see you, and you won't see them. Turn on the workplace shield to hide from anyone who lists the same company.

How does the reveal work?

Every chat starts in dark mode with blurred photos. Then it opens in steps: one photo each, all photos, then contact details. In a match between a man and a woman, she approves every step. Phone numbers and social handles are blocked in chat until the Contact step.

Why don't I see more people?

Swoosh shows only verified people who fit your filters and whose filters you fit, in the same mode. That's fewer people than swipe apps, and far fewer wasted chats. Widen your age range or allow other cities to see more.

My city isn't live yet. Can I join?

Yes. Create and verify your profile now, and you'll get picks the day your city opens.
