How PAUSE works

Slow is the point.

Verify

One ID photo and a selfie, checked by a real person. We delete both after the check. One ID, one account.

Pick a category

Steady or Forever. Switch Masked and Destiny on or off whenever you like.

Talk & play

A handful of picks a day, not endless swiping. Break the ice with This or That or 2 truths, 1 lie.

Unmask together

Photos, then numbers — each step only when you both say yes. Then meet somewhere public.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is PAUSE free?

Yes, while we launch in Bangalore and Delhi NCR. If we add paid features later, you’ll see the price before anything is charged.

Who can see my photos?

With Masked on, nobody — until you and the other person both tap Unmask. With Masked off, verified people in your category can see them. Every photo shown to someone else is watermarked with their account code.

Why only 5 chats?

Because 5 real conversations beat 50 half-ones. It’s the same for everyone — men and women. Close a chat politely and a new slot opens.

What happens to my ID?

A real person checks your ID photo and selfie, then both are deleted. We keep only a verified tick and a one-way code of your ID number, so one ID can’t make two accounts.

Do I need a kundli?

No. Kundli is optional and only used in Forever. Destiny is optional too — you can use PAUSE without either.

Can my family or colleagues see me?

Not if you don’t want them to. Add their numbers or your company to the Contact shield and you’ll never see each other.

Ready to slow down?

Verified people. Five real conversations. Unmask when you both say so.
